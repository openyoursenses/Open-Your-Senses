Why I created Open Your Senses

Open Your Senses started with my son. He has Asperger's, and school was incredibly difficult for him. For years, I felt I was trying to explain something that nobody was really hearing.

I repeatedly asked for him to be assessed because I could see that he was struggling. Three long years later, we finally had an answer. An answer didn't suddenly make everything easy, but it helped us understand him.

Along the way, we began discovering things that could help him feel more comfortable and navigate the world around him. Finding the right sensory tools wasn't easy: there were so many products, websites and pieces of information to work through.

My son is now an adult, but I haven't forgotten how it felt to be the parent searching for answers. That's why I created Open Your Senses: a place where parents, carers, teachers, professionals and individuals can explore sensory tools without feeling overwhelmed.

“I'm not sure what I need. Can you help me?”

Of course.

Sensory needs don't belong to one age, one diagnosis or one type of person. We all experience the world through our senses. We can't promise the perfect answer for everyone, but we can start with the person, not the label.

Common Questions & Gentle Answers

I don't know what my loved one needs. Where should I start?

You don't have to be an expert. Explore tools around everyday sensory experiences such as calm, focus, movement and tactile play. If you're unsure, get in touch and we'll do our best to point you towards options worth exploring.

Are weighted plushies suitable for everyone?

Weighted products aren't suitable for everyone. Read the manufacturer's age, weight and safety guidance carefully and ensure the person can remove the item independently. If unsure, seek appropriate professional advice.

How do I clean and care for sensory tools?

Care instructions vary by product. Follow the manufacturer's cleaning guidance supplied with each item, and contact us if you're unsure.

How can I ask for help?

Email us with a little information about what you're looking for. We can help you explore the options available, though every person's sensory preferences are different.

Need a little help?

Not sure where to begin? That's completely okay. Tell us what you're hoping to support, and we'll help you explore the options we have.

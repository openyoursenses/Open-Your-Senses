Why I created Open Your Senses

Open Your Senses started with my son.

He has Asperger's, and school was incredibly difficult for him.

For years, I felt like I was trying to explain something that nobody was really hearing.

I repeatedly asked for him to be assessed because I could see that he was struggling. But again and again, I was told that he didn't meet the criteria.

Three long years later, we finally had an answer: Asperger's.

An answer didn't suddenly make everything easy, but it helped us understand him.

We worked hard to find the right educational setting and, along the way, began discovering the things that could help him feel more comfortable, regulated and able to navigate the world around him.

But there was another problem.

Finding the right sensory tools was a nightmare.

There were so many products, so many different websites and so much information to work through. It was difficult to know what was genuinely useful, what might suit his needs and where to even begin.

My son is now an adult.

But I haven't forgotten how it felt to be the parent searching for answers, trying to find something — anything — that might make life a little easier.

That's why I created Open Your Senses.

I wanted to create the place I wish I'd had back then.

A place where parents, carers, teachers, therapists, professionals and individuals can come to explore sensory tools and experiences without feeling overwhelmed.

A place where you don't have to be an expert.

A place where different needs are understood and respected.

And a place where someone can simply say:

“I'm not sure what I need. Can you help me?”

And the answer is:

“Of course.”

Because sensory needs don't belong to one age, one diagnosis or one type of person. They are part of being human.

From babies discovering the world around them, to children finding ways to regulate, adults finding focus and comfort, and older people reconnecting with familiar sights, sounds, textures and memories — there is always room to explore our senses.

I can't promise that we'll have the perfect answer for everyone. But I can promise that Open Your Senses will always start with understanding the person, not the label.

Because everyone deserves to be heard.

Common Questions & Gentle Answers

Everything you might want to know about our sensory tools, safety, and finding the right fit for your loved one.

🔍 I don't know what my loved one needs. Where should I start?

Please don't worry—you do not have to be an expert here. We deliberately organize our tools by experiences (like calm, focus, or communication) rather than clinical terms. If you feel completely lost, scroll to the bottom of this page and use our contact form. Tell us a little bit about what your child, student, or relative is finding difficult, and we will happily help you guide your choices.

🧸 Are the weighted plushies suitable for everyone?

Weighted tools provide wonderful "deep pressure" therapy that mimics a firm, grounding hug. As a general safety rule for children and seniors, any weighted item should never weigh more than 10% of the individual's total body weight, and the user must always be able to easily remove the item off themselves independently.

🧼 How do I clean and care for these sensory tools?

Because these tools are meant to be deeply loved and actively used, cleanliness is important. Our medical-grade silicone oral chews and pencil toppers can be easily washed with warm soapy water or popped in a sterilizer. For weighted plushies and canvas boxes, we recommend gentle surface spot-cleaning with a damp cloth to protect the sensory fillings inside.

🏫 Can you fulfill bulk orders or invoices for schools and care homes?

Yes, absolutely. We love supporting local UK nurseries, classrooms, therapists, and residential memory care homes. If you need a custom package built for a specific budget or requires specialized invoicing paperwork for your local authority, simply drop us an email via the form below and we will get it sorted for you.
